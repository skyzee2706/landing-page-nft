Who Are the Chilliens?

Chilliens is a collection of 4,444 unique Chill Aliens built on Robinhood Chain. More than a collectible — it's an ecosystem where NFT holders participate through a self-sustaining sweep, burn, and giveaway mechanism driven purely by trading activity.

Every Chillian has its own identity, personality, and appearance — generated from a wide set of traits including alien colors, backgrounds, eyes, mouths, headwear, necklaces, and accessories.

FAQ

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about Chilliens and the ecosystem.

What is the total supply of Chilliens? +

The launch supply is 4,444 unique NFTs. However, through the Sweep & Burn mechanism, the effective circulating supply will continuously decrease over time — targeting approximately ~2,500 NFTs as the long-term supply.

What are the mint prices? +

There are three mint tiers:

  • GTD (Guaranteed): Free — for whitelisted wallets
  • FCFS (First Come, First Served): 0.00007 ETH
  • Public: 0.00012 ETH

Secure a GTD whitelist spot to mint for free!

Which blockchain is Chilliens on? +

Chilliens is built on Robinhood Chain — an EVM-compatible network. Make sure your wallet supports Robinhood Chain before minting.

How does the Sweep, Burn & Giveaway mechanic work? +

100% of Creator Fees from secondary-market trading are split into two pools:

  • 50% → Sweep & Burn: NFTs are bought from the floor and permanently burned, reducing total supply.
  • 50% → Community Giveaway: Swept NFTs are redistributed back to the community through giveaways.

This creates a self-sustaining deflationary and redistributive ecosystem driven entirely by trading activity.

When does the mint open? +

Mint date is TBA. Follow @ChilliensNFT on X for the official announcement. The countdown on this page will update the moment it's confirmed.

What are the benefits of holding a Chillien? +

As a holder you benefit from:

  • Participation in the NFT Giveaway redistribution pool
  • Exposure to supply reduction over time as burns decrease circulating NFTs
  • Full trading rights on any EVM-compatible marketplace
  • Community events and holder-focused features in later phases
